title: "Joomla support deprecated"
sidebar:
  hidden: true
---

Joomla support has been deprecated as of **June 18, 2026**. Patchstack App and the Patchstack connector support **WordPress** and **Drupal** only.

As of **August 2026**, Joomla is also removed from the public vulnerability database search, VDP platform filters, and related partner tooling. Existing Joomla catalog entries were purged; new Joomla sites and products are not accepted.

If you were previously using the Patchstack connector for Joomla, no further action is needed. If you have any questions, reach out to us via live chat at the bottom right corner.
