Your data, and how we handle it, should never be a mystery. Everything you need for a GDPR review lives on patchstack.com, where we keep it current, so the documents below are the single source of truth.

## Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Need a DPA on file? Download our pre-signed Data Processing Addendum, countersign it, and email it to privacy@patchstack.com.

[Read and download the DPA](https://patchstack.com/dpa/)

## Privacy Policy

Our Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

[Read the Privacy Policy](https://patchstack.com/privacy-policy/)

## Terms and Conditions

The Terms and Conditions cover the rules of using Patchstack's services, for you and for us.

[Read the Terms and Conditions](https://patchstack.com/terms-and-conditions/)

Still have a question about privacy or compliance? Write to us at privacy@patchstack.com and we'll help.
